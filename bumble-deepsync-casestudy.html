02 · Reframing the Opportunity

From immediacy to meaningful compatibility.

The original ask was straightforward: notify users when a match was within 0.5 km. But rather than treating proximity as the solution, I examined the assumption behind it—whether creating more immediacy would actually lead to stronger connections.

The data suggested otherwise. While proximity could prompt faster action, engagement declined over time. Speeding up the path to meeting wasn't addressing the underlying issue; it was simply accelerating interactions that might not have enough compatibility to sustain them.

This shifted the focus from how quickly people connect to whether a connection has enough substance to continue.

03 · The Conversational Cost

Swiping is easy.
Asking what matters isn't.

Most dating experiences optimize for getting people to a match quickly, while early conversations stay on easy topics like weekend plans, food, and music. Yet the signals that matter more for long-term compatibility—values, conflict style, emotional needs, stability, and independence—are often harder to surface naturally.

Understanding these signals earlier can help people decide whether a connection is worth investing in before weeks of momentum build around a mismatch. This shaped DeepSync around a simple idea: surface high-value compatibility signals earlier, while lowering the social friction of exploring them together.

Rather than adding another compatibility score—or turning early conversations into an interview—DeepSync structures harder questions into a shared experience, then turns what both people reveal into something they can actually talk about.

The goal isn't to rush intimacy—it's to help people invest their time more intentionally.

04 · Designing for Co-Creation

Compatibility shouldn't be something the app simply tells you. It can be something two people discover together.

The sprint lets each match participate independently, while shared insights only unlock once both people respond. This creates mutual investment before any AI-generated insight appears.

Asynchronous Participation

Each person responds on their own time. No pressure to be online simultaneously.

Shared Unlock

Insights only appear once both people complete their responses. Neither sees results alone.

06 · Designing AI as a Conversation Catalyst

The AI doesn't replace the conversation. It creates the conditions for a better one.

DeepSync compares what both people explicitly shared, identifies a meaningful point of alignment or difference, and uses AI to generate a grounded Conversation Spark. The system then hands control back to the user, who decides whether to send it.

"Free helps people talk. Premium helps them understand the pattern."

Rather than gating the shared sprint upfront, DeepSync uses the free experience to drive repeated participation and post-match conversation. By Day 3, both people have contributed enough signal for the deeper interpretation to feel specific—and curiosity about the relationship has had time to build.

Premium captures that higher-intent moment by unlocking the full DeepSync Report.

08 · Validate Before Expanding

Does the ritual create better conversation—or just another task?

Before expanding DeepSync into a broader compatibility system, I'd first validate whether the shared ritual actually changes post-match behavior—not just whether people complete it.
