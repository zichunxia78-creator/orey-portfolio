Learnvia AI tutor · CMU × Learnvia, 2026

Showed that scaffolding beats answers: 38% vs. 14% learner recovery

Role
Design lead
Team
5 designers, with Learnvia
Time
Jan–Aug 2026 · 8 months

The decision

We narrowed a larger codebook to five learner behaviors that raters could code reliably (α ≈ 0.82), then mapped 21,891 turn transitions.

What it cost

Coarser codes. Behaviors raters couldn’t agree on were left out, so the tutor only reacts to what we could measure.

Source-grounded answer verifier, 2026

A source-checking pass cut unsupported claims from 2 to 0

Role
Solo: experiment, pipeline, evaluation
Team
Solo
Time
Summer 2026

The decision

Verify the exact direct draft instead of adding a planning step, so any change can be traced to verification alone.

What it cost

One regression: the verifier made a strong answer worse (11.5 → 10.5), so it isn’t worth running on every question.
